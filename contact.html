Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we receive most often. Click any question to expand the full answer.

How quickly will CalcMyPay respond to my email?

We aim to respond to every message within a few business days. Urgent issues like calculator errors are prioritised and addressed as quickly as possible. We read every email we receive, though high volume sometimes means responses take slightly longer during peak periods.

Is CalcMyPay affiliated with SARS or the government?

No. CalcMyPay is a completely independent platform. We build our calculators using publicly available SARS tax tables and verify figures carefully each tax year, but we have no affiliation with SARS, the Department of Employment and Labour, or any government body. For official matters like filing returns or UIF claims, use official government channels directly.

Can CalcMyPay give me personalised financial or tax advice?

No. CalcMyPay provides free educational tools and general guidance, not personalised financial, tax, or legal advice. For advice tailored to your specific situation, consult a registered financial adviser (FSCA-registered) or a registered tax practitioner (SARS-registered).

How accurate are the CalcMyPay calculators?

Every tax calculation is built directly from official SARS 2027 tax year brackets, rebates, and thresholds. We verify figures against SARS publications and update whenever legislation changes. Calculators provide accurate estimates for planning, but actual tax positions can vary based on specific circumstances a general calculator cannot capture.

Is my financial information stored when I use a calculator?

No. Every calculation happens entirely within your browser. The numbers you enter are never transmitted to our servers, stored, or seen by anyone. You can use every tool on CalcMyPay without creating an account or providing any personal information beyond what you choose to type into a calculator.

I found an error in a calculation — what should I do?

Please email hello@calcmypay.co.za with: 1) Which calculator you used, 2) The exact numbers you entered, 3) What result you expected and why. We take accuracy seriously and prioritise fixing genuine errors as quickly as possible.

Can I suggest a new calculator or tool?

Yes, we genuinely welcome tool suggestions. CalcMyPay grows based on what South Africans actually need to plan their finances. Email us with what you are looking for, why it would be useful, and how you would use it. Tool requests directly shape our development priorities.

Are your tax figures up to date for 2026?

Yes, all calculators use the current SARS 2027 tax year figures (1 March 2026 to 28 February 2027), verified against official SARS publications. We review and update figures whenever SARS announces changes or new tax tables are released.

Does CalcMyPay have a mobile app or API?

Not currently. CalcMyPay is a web-based platform optimised for mobile browsers. A mobile app and API are both under consideration based on user demand — email us if either would be useful to you.

Can CalcMyPay help me with my SARS return or UIF claim?

No. While our tools can help you understand your tax position, we cannot assist with filing returns or submitting UIF claims. For SARS matters, use SARS eFiling or call 0800 00 7277. For UIF matters, use uFiling (ufiling.labour.gov.za).
