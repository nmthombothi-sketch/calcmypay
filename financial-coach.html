How the AI Financial Coach actually works

A rules-based coach, built on real financial planning principles

This tool analyses your income, expenses, debt and savings against widely-used financial planning benchmarks — the same guidelines used by financial advisers — to produce a health score and specific recommendations. It is not a generic chatbot; every number and suggestion it gives is calculated directly from what you enter.

The four pillars behind your score

  • Savings rate — how much of your income you are keeping and putting to work, benchmarked against the widely recommended 15% target.
  • Debt burden — how much of your income is committed to servicing debt, and whether that leaves room for anything else.
  • Emergency fund — how many months of expenses you could cover if your income stopped tomorrow.
  • Retirement readiness — whether your current trajectory is realistically on track for the retirement you want.

What makes the recommendations specific to you

Rather than generic advice like "save more" or "spend less", the coach calculates exact figures — how much more to save, what your emergency fund target actually is in rands, what a specific change would do to your position — based on the numbers you provide, processed entirely in your browser.

Your privacy while using this tool

None of the financial details you enter here are transmitted to or stored on any server. The analysis happens locally on your device and disappears when you close or refresh the page.

Why a single score can meaningfully represent complex finances

Financial health involves many moving parts — income, spending, debt, savings, and long-term planning — which can feel overwhelming to assess individually. A composite score, built from the metrics that matter most, gives you a fast, honest read on your overall position, similar to how a credit score condenses complex repayment history into a single usable number.

Why the same score can mean different things for different people

A moderate score for someone in their twenties, just beginning to build financial habits, represents a very different situation than the same score for someone approaching retirement age. Context matters — use the score as a snapshot of your current trajectory, and revisit it periodically to track whether your trajectory is improving.

The value of the scenario simulator specifically

The most actionable part of this tool is not the score itself, but seeing concretely how a specific, achievable change — an extra R500/month in savings, a small increase in retirement contribution — shifts your outlook. Abstract advice to "save more" rarely motivates action; seeing the calculated effect of a specific number often does.

What this tool intentionally does not do

This coach provides educational analysis based on the numbers you enter, using established financial planning principles — it does not access your actual accounts, provide regulated financial advice, or replace a conversation with a qualified financial adviser for complex situations like estate planning, tax structuring, or investment product selection.

Making the most of your results

Revisit this tool every few months as your situation changes, and use it alongside our specific calculators for deeper analysis — the retirement calculator for long-term projections, or the debt repayment calculator if debt is holding your score back.
