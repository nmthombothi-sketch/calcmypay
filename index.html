South Africa's money questions, answered honestly

Most South Africans reach a point where the standard financial advice — "just save more," "just budget better" — stops being useful, because it does not answer the specific question in front of them: what will I actually take home this month? Can I really afford this house? Am I on track for retirement, or dangerously behind? CalcMyPay exists to answer those specific questions with real numbers, not generic advice.

Built on the current SARS tax year, not outdated assumptions

Every calculation on this platform — from your basic salary after tax to complex scenarios involving retirement contributions, medical aid credits and travel allowances — is built on the current SARS tax year figures, checked directly against official SARS publications. Tax rules change; we update the underlying calculations when they do, so the numbers you see reflect the actual rules currently in force.

From a single number to a complete financial picture

A salary calculation is often just the starting point. Once you know your real take-home pay, the natural next questions follow: what does that mean for what you can afford? Are you saving enough? What happens if you change jobs, take on debt, or want to retire early? CalcMyPay is built so that every calculator connects to the next relevant question — your take-home pay links directly to affordability tools, your affordability numbers link to your broader budget, and your budget links to your long-term wealth trajectory.

Privacy by design, not as an afterthought

Every number you enter into any calculator on this site is processed entirely within your own browser. Nothing is transmitted to a server, stored in a database, or associated with your identity in any way. You can use every tool here — including the AI financial coach — with complete privacy, using only the numbers you choose to enter.

How the five categories work together

CalcMyPay is built around a simple idea: your financial life is connected, and your tools should be too. Most South African calculator sites give you one number in isolation — a salary figure, a bond estimate — and leave you to work out what it means for everything else. This platform is deliberately structured so each category feeds into the next.

Salary & Income is the starting point for almost every other decision. Once you know your real take-home pay, Affordability tools translate that into what house, car or rent genuinely fits your budget. Wealth Building tools then look further ahead — how that same income, saved and invested consistently, compounds into retirement security or financial independence. Career Intelligence lets you benchmark your income against real South African data, so you know whether a raise, a career change, or further study is likely to move the needle. And Money Tools ties it together day to day, with budgeting, debt payoff and emergency fund planning that keep the whole plan on track.

Why we built an AI financial coach on top

Individual calculators are useful, but most people do not think in isolated numbers — they think in questions like "am I doing okay?" or "what should I focus on first?" The financial coach exists to answer exactly that, pulling together your income, expenses, debt and savings into a single health score with specific, prioritised next steps, rather than leaving you to manually cross-reference six different tools.

Built for South African realities

We understand that South African finances come with unique challenges: high unemployment, expensive credit, medical aid costs, and the reality that many people support extended family. Our calculators account for these realities, and our guides address the specific questions South Africans face — from understanding your payslip to planning for retirement in an uncertain economy.

Our commitment to accuracy

Every calculator on CalcMyPay is built and tested against real-world scenarios. We cross-reference our calculations with SARS publications, industry standards, and financial planning best practices. When tax tables change (as they do annually), we update our tools within days, not months. We also encourage users to report any discrepancies they find — our community helps us maintain the highest standards of accuracy.

Free forever, no strings attached

CalcMyPay is and will always be completely free. We don't sell your data, we don't require sign-ups, and we don't have premium tiers. We believe financial literacy and planning tools should be accessible to every South African, regardless of income level. Our platform is supported by non-intrusive advertising that doesn't compromise your privacy or experience.
