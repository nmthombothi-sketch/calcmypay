Privacy Policy

How we handle your data — and why your financial information never leaves your browser.

Last updated: August 2026 POPIA Compliant ✓ Browser-only processing

🔒 Privacy Promise — The Short Version

  • Your financial data never leaves your browser. All calculators run locally using JavaScript.
  • We don't store your salary, expenses, or debt figures. When you close the page, they're gone.
  • We collect minimal analytics — anonymous, aggregated usage data to improve the site.
  • We use Google AdSense for advertising — you can opt out of personalised ads.
  • We are fully POPIA compliant — South Africa's data protection law.

1. Introduction

CalcMyPay ("we", "us", "our") operates calcmypay.co.za, a South African personal finance platform providing free calculators, tools, and educational content. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under South African law.

By using our Site, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the Site.

2. Browser-Only Processing: Your Financial Data Stays With You

This is the most important section of our privacy policy, and what differentiates us from most financial platforms:

Every calculator on CalcMyPay runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript. The financial figures you enter — your salary, expenses, debt balances, savings, investments, property values — are processed locally on your device. They are never transmitted to our servers, never stored in any database, and never accessible to us in any form.

What this means practically

  • No server storage: Your inputs exist only in your browser's memory during your session
  • No transmission: We don't send your numbers anywhere — not to us, not to third parties
  • No database: We have no database of user financial information because we never collect it
  • No account required: You can use all our tools without creating an account or providing an email
  • No history: We cannot see your previous calculations — each session starts fresh

Why we chose this architecture

Financial data is sensitive. By processing everything locally, we eliminate an entire category of privacy risk. Even if our servers were compromised, attackers would find no user financial data — because it was never there. This architecture isn't just privacy-friendly; it's privacy-by-design.

7. Security Measures

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect any information we process:

Technical safeguards

  • HTTPS encryption: All traffic to our Site is encrypted using TLS 1.3
  • Secure hosting: Infrastructure hosted with reputable providers with SOC 2 compliance
  • Regular updates: Software and dependencies kept current to patch vulnerabilities
  • Access controls: Limited personnel access to any collected data
  • Monitoring: Security monitoring for unusual activity or attacks

Architectural protection

The strongest security measure is our browser-only architecture. Since your financial data never reaches our servers, it cannot be:

  • Stolen in a data breach (it's not there)
  • Accessed by unauthorised employees (we don't have it)
  • Subpoenaed or legally compelled from us (we can't produce what we don't possess)
  • Accidentally exposed in backups (it was never stored)
Security reality: No system is perfectly secure, but our architecture means that even a worst-case breach of our systems would expose no user financial data — because that data was never on our systems to begin with.

10. Your Rights Under POPIA

South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) gives you specific rights regarding your personal information:

10.1 Your rights

  • Right to access: Request a copy of personal information we hold about you
  • Right to correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to deletion: Request deletion of your personal information ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to object: Object to processing of your information, including for direct marketing
  • Right to withdraw consent: Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Right to data portability: Receive your information in a machine-readable format
  • Right to complain: Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator

10.2 How to exercise your rights

To exercise any of these rights:

  1. Email our Information Officer at privacy@calcmypay.co.za
  2. Clearly state which right you wish to exercise
  3. Provide sufficient information for us to identify you (e.g., email address used in contact form)
  4. We will respond within 30 days as required by POPIA

10.3 Practical reality

Because we collect minimal personal information — primarily anonymous analytics rather than identifiable profiles — most POPIA rights requests will relate to cookie and browser-level data rather than a personal profile. In many cases, you can exercise these rights directly through your browser settings or the opt-out links provided above.

Example: If you want to "delete" your information, you can simply clear your browser cookies and cache. This removes all analytics cookies and any locally-stored preferences, effectively erasing your footprint on our Site.

17. Frequently Asked Questions

Does CalcMyPay store my financial data?

No. All our calculators run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your salary, expenses, debt, savings, and other financial figures are never transmitted to our servers and never stored by us. When you close or refresh the page, all data is gone.

Is CalcMyPay POPIA compliant?

Yes. CalcMyPay is fully compliant with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). We collect minimal personal information, process it lawfully, and respect your data subject rights including access, correction, and objection to processing.

What cookies does CalcMyPay use?

We use three types of cookies: essential cookies for basic site functionality, analytics cookies (Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site, and advertising cookies (Google AdSense) to serve relevant ads. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Can I opt out of personalised advertising?

Yes. You can opt out of personalised Google advertising at Google Ads Settings or through the Network Advertising Initiative at aboutads.info. You can also disable cookies entirely in your browser settings.

Who is CalcMyPay's Information Officer?

Our Information Officer can be contacted at privacy@calcmypay.co.za. Under POPIA, the Information Officer is responsible for ensuring compliance with the Act and handling data subject requests.

How do I exercise my POPIA rights?

Email privacy@calcmypay.co.za with your request. You have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of your personal information. We will respond within 30 days as required by POPIA.

Does CalcMyPay share my data with third parties?

We never share financial data because we never collect it. For the limited analytics data we do collect (anonymous, aggregated usage statistics), we use Google Analytics and Google AdSense who have their own privacy policies and compliance frameworks.

Where is my data stored?

Your financial data is never stored — it exists only in your browser session. Anonymous analytics data may be processed in the United States or European Union by Google, under standard contractual clauses and appropriate safeguards.
